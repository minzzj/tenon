The ShareAlike condition applies **when adapted material is shared/distributed** to others. Pure internal modifications that are never distributed cannot be forced to be published by this or any other license. If the goal is to guarantee that the whole ecosystem stays open, the practical mechanism is publishing forks publicly under the same license.
